How to Use CSV Viewer Online

A fast and lightweight online spreadsheet viewer designed for immediate file inspection and quick edits.

1

Select or drop your file

Click Browse file or drag your .csv, .tsv, or .json file into the box. You can also paste copied data directly with Ctrl+V.

2

Sort, search, and edit cells

Click any column title to sort from lowest to highest or vice versa. Type in the search box to filter rows instantly. Click on any cell to edit its value directly.

3

Download your results

Save your changes by clicking Download CSV or Download JSON. Your downloaded file is created directly on your device.

Privacy & Security Guarantee

Open, edit, sort, and search your file. Nothing is uploaded — it is parsed on your own device.

Unlike ordinary web apps that upload files to a cloud server to convert or render them, CSV Viewer Online processes 100% of your data using your browser's native JavaScript engine and HTML5 FileReader API. You can test this by disconnecting your internet connection: the entire viewer, editor, and exporter will continue working without interruption.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data uploaded to any server?

No. All parsing and viewing happens 100% locally on your computer inside your browser tab. Your files and data never touch any remote server, database, or analytics tracker.

Can I edit data and download the updated file?

Yes. You can click any cell to edit its value directly, add new rows, sort columns, and click Download CSV or Download JSON to save your changes immediately.

What file types can I open?

You can open CSV files (comma-separated), TSV files (tab-separated), semicolon-separated spreadsheets, and standard JSON arrays.

Does this work offline?

Yes. Once the page is loaded, you can disconnect from the internet and continue opening, editing, searching, and exporting files completely offline.
